OUR MISSION

PLANTERRAX is an educational farming simulation game that bridges the gap between complex scientific data and practical agricultural applications. We empower players to make data-driven farming decisions using real NASA satellite imagery and climate data.

Our goal is to demonstrate how modern technology can revolutionize agriculture by optimizing crop yields, reducing resource consumption, and minimizing environmental impact.

⚡

THE CHALLENGE

Agriculture faces unprecedented challenges as the world population grows and climate patterns shift. While advanced technologies like satellite imagery and climate modeling exist, there is a significant knowledge gap in their accessibility and application, especially for smaller farms.

PLANTERRAX addresses this by creating an engaging, educational platform that makes NASA's wealth of real-time data on climate conditions, soil moisture, and vegetation health accessible to everyone.

EDUCATIONAL IMPACT

PLANTERRAX transforms complex scientific data into an accessible learning experience. Through realistic farming scenarios, players develop practical skills in data analysis and sustainable agriculture.

By simulating the challenges faced by real farmers, we help players understand how technology can address global food security while preserving our planet's resources for future generations.

🛰️

POWERED BY NASA

Our game leverages NASA's open data initiative, incorporating datasets that provide real-time information on:

  • Climate conditions and weather forecasting
  • Soil moisture at various depths
  • Vegetation health and growth patterns
  • Temperature variations and seasonal trends
  • Precipitation patterns and water availability
